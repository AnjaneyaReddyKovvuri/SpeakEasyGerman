    Install on this device

    AI tutor

    Choose which AI service plays Lena, writes the audios, scores your answers and translates. Speech recognition and the German voice are not affected by this.

    Speech recognition (optional)

    Normally the phone's own speech recognition is used. With a Groq (free tier) or OpenAI key, Whisper can be used instead: set “Speech” at the top to “Whisper”. It works in every browser and lets you replay your own recording in the Pronunciation tab. If your AI service above is already Groq or OpenAI, its key is used and you can leave this empty.
